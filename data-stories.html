One Facility for Every Eighteen Deaths

Eleven years of federal overdose mortality, 612,499 deaths, set against every treatment facility in SAMHSA's national locator. Whether the states carrying the heaviest burden hold the capacity to match, and where that breaks down.

  • CDC WONDER, SAMHSA, Census ACS
  • 612,499 deaths
  • 2014–2024
How it was built
  • Merged two CDC WONDER databases into a single 2014–2024 series, validated to the death against published NCHS totals.
  • Tiered SAMHSA's 7,756 facilities by whether they can dispense methadone.
  • Rank-correlated burden against capacity across 51 jurisdictions, then isolated the widest rank gaps.
  • Cross-checked against deaths per facility to catch small-state artifacts.

Python, pandas, SciPy, Plotly, Requests, HTML/CSS, JavaScript

02

New York City's Quietest Emergency

Eight years of NYC 911 mental health crisis calls, 1.35 million of them. Where the burden actually falls once precinct population is accounted for, and how it lines up with the city's clinician-response coverage.

  • NYC Open Data, NYPD
  • 1,348,385 incidents
  • 2018–2025
How it was built
  • Pulled 2.59M NYC 911 dispatch rows in year-partitioned, resumable batches with per-year verification counts.
  • Deduplicated to 1,348,385 EDP incidents on the CAD event id.
  • Spatially joined 2020 census blocks to 78 precincts for per-resident baselines.
  • Separated the fall in calls from the fall in units dispatched per call.

Python, pandas, NumPy, GeoPandas, Plotly, HTML/CSS, JavaScript

The Quiet Math of NYC's Restaurant Inspections

An analytical look at ~83,000 NYC health inspections across ~27,000 restaurants. What an A grade actually signals, where the system bends around the grade boundary, and how few kitchens hold a clean inspection history.

  • NYC Open Data, DOHMH
  • 83,354 inspections
  • 2022–2026
How it was built
  • Collapsed 296,393 DOHMH violation rows into 83,354 inspections across 27,350 restaurants.
  • Windowed to mid-2022 onward, where the rolling three-year file reaches full volume.
  • Measured closure rates by violation code against the citywide baseline.
  • Traced score distribution at the A/B boundary and re-inspection recovery over time.

Python, pandas, NumPy, Plotly, Pillow, HTML/CSS, JavaScript
