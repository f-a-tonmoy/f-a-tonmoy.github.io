New York City's Quietest Emergency

  • Pulled 2.59M NYC 911 dispatch rows in year-partitioned, resumable batches with per-year verification counts, deduplicated to 1,348,385 EDP crisis incidents (2018-2025), and spatially joined census blocks to precincts for per-resident baselines.
  • Examined where crisis burden falls once precinct population is accounted for, and whether the raw decline reflects fewer people in crisis or a change in how calls are dispatched.
  • Broke the eight-year window down by hour, weekday, and season. Measured how unevenly volume spreads across the city's 78 precincts.

Python, pandas, NumPy, GeoPandas, Plotly, HTML/CSS, JavaScript

The Quiet Math of NYC's Restaurant Inspections

  • Built a reproducible ETL pipeline, collapsing 296,393 NYC DOHMH violation rows into 83,354 inspections across 27,350 restaurants.
  • Investigated what an A grade actually signals, what the pile-ups at the passing ('A') grade boundary indicate, and which violations trigger on-the-spot closures.
  • Traced how quickly failing kitchens recover, how cuisines cluster geographically, and how few restaurants hold a clean inspection history.
